- Hardcore Grind & Risk: Classic high-stakes progression, open-world dynamics, and real penalties for death.
- Deep Customization: Path of Exile-inspired skill trees and item modifier pools layered on top of classic Lineage 2 class/archetype structures.
- Meaningful Exploration & Progression: Bestiary-driven skill point acquisition, dynamic quest design, and high-tier crafting/gearing.
- Architecture: Driven by two core relational tables:
  - Quest Stages & Tasks Table: Defines flexible branching steps and objectives.
  - Quest Rewards Table: Handles reward distributions based on stage completion.
- Dynamic / Shared Quest Starters:
  - Quests can share entry points across players/factions (e.g., Starting Region Blacksmith).
  - Quest steps progress dynamically, ending with guaranteed directional rewards (e.g., location/clues pointing to specific world objectives).
- Mana System: Mana management is a core resource gate for all combat roles.
- Buff System: Persistent and temporary buff mechanics play a major role in party efficiency and solo survivability.
- Races: Multiple distinct races providing foundational stat distributions and traits.
- Archetypes & Classes:
  - Melee / Physical Fighters: Frontline damage dealers.
  - Tanks: Aggro management and mitigation specialists.
  - Mages / Ranged Spells: AoE and single-target elemental/arcane damage.
  - Support / Priests: Healers, purifiers, and defensive mitigation.
  - Shamans (Buffers): Dedicated stat-enhancers and party enablers.
- Hard Cap: Maximum of 50 Skill Points total across the character’s life.
- Starter Abilities: Every character begins with 5 basic skill slots:
  1. Melee Attack
  2. Cast Attack
  3. Defend / Block
  4. Movement
  5. Support
- Slot Unlocks: Additional active skill slots unlock as the character advances in level and milestone progression (unlimited total pool potential, but restricted equipped active slots).
- Evolution System: Enhancing a skill presents a choice of 3 distinct evolution paths per tier/stage.
- Tier Progression Rules:
  - Max Evolutions: Up to 10 total evolutions per individual skill tree.
  - Soft Resets / Tier Spreading: You cannot rush straight down a single path to level 10 (X-1 -> X-10). Progression requires evolving cross-branch nodes (e.g., balancing branches X, Y, and Z).
  - Respec Flexibility: Players can soft-reset/re-path individual evolution choices (e.g., swapping node 7 out of 10 without resetting the entire character).
Skill points are not gained simply by leveling up; they are awarded through Bestiary Completion (first-kill bonuses per monster tier per region):
- Best-in-Slot (BiS) equipment drops primarily from World Bosses.
- Modifiers draw from two pools: General Modifiers (universal stats) and Item-Specific Modifiers (class/skill-altering stats).
- Dying inflicts durability loss on equipped items.
- Durability loss per item is randomized in amount and targeted items, scaled relative to item power level.
- Sub-50% Durability: Items reaching below 50% durability become Exposed. Exposed items carry a percentage chance to drop on player death.
- 0% Durability: Items reaching 0% durability automatically drop on death.
- Crafting allows players to craft high-tier gear using scalable resource inputs to accommodate varying budgets.
- Base Crafting Example (Sword of Caca):
  - Base Materials: 100 Wood + 100 Iron
